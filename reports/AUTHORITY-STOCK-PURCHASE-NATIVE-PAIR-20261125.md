# Native Linux Company stock purchase

On 25 November 2026, the regular Linux Company pilot and native provider ran
as two X4 9.00 instances on one Bazzite host. Each used its own private
profile and display. The client joined through the provider's pinned
loopback TLS endpoint. The trial used revision `0e94cec4`. A private driver
operated the registered Stock Trade widgets after admission. It invoked
the active Confirm callback once, with physical mouse input outside the
test scope.

The client boarded WRN-302 (registry index 7354) with its assigned captain
PYN-740 and then gained the helm. The seat's read-only berth result matched
an independent native read at BIM-169 in Grand Exchange I, with bay seed
`507326418098681806`. The provider separately verified its native dock
binding and booked the stock order.

## Purchase and departure

The client and provider quoted 58.48 Cr per unit. The stock basket for
two water displayed a total of 116.00 Cr. The provider's native journal recorded
that debit, and the admitted TLS connection returned a confirmed receipt
for purchase `6a6cc2e65bd0dbf6150108c3c8a80009`. The cockpit showed
9,768.00 Cr and four water in the confirmation notice. The pilot's
independent local read still showed 9,884.00 Cr and two water after the
notice, the same values it read before Confirm.
